Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's — AI

The question I want to carry into AI is simple: what survives when the distribution changes?

My strongest preparation is not a finished robotics system. It's the repeated experience of testing models across people, finding where they fail, and tracing the representation problem behind it. EMAI gives that work a broader AI and intelligent-systems setting.

Why this programme

What survives when the training distribution changes.

The research problem

Models that look reliable on familiar data often degrade the moment the input distribution shifts — a new subject, a new session, a new acquisition setup. My work treats that shift as the object of study, not noise to average away.

What has already been tested

  • A MiniROCKET domain-shift mechanism study
  • A protocol-locked learned-invariance direction (Lite-DAN)
  • A systems-integration layer connecting decoding research to simulated control

Why AI, and why now

I need more systematic, formal training in representation learning, adaptation, and intelligent decision-making than an independent research programme can provide on its own.

What I want to study

The EMAI mobility track most aligned with robotics or intelligent decision-making, to study robust representations and apply them to human-centred intelligent systems.
