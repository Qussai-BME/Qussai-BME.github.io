Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's — Medical Imaging

The question that would carry over from signals to images.

My current work is on biosignals — sEMG and EEG. MAIA's modality is medical imaging: a genuine shift, not a small step, and one I want to make honestly rather than imply I've already made.

Why this programme

What stays the same when the modality changes.

What stays the same

The question underneath all of my work: does a model actually generalise to a subject it never saw, or does it just look accurate on familiar data? That question doesn't depend on the signal being electrical.

What changes, and why I'm saying so directly

Medical imaging is a real shift from my current biosignal work, not a natural extension I want to understate.

What already transfers

  • Biomedical engineering and biological sensing coursework
  • Python, machine learning, signal processing, and experimental design
  • An open, reproducible research practice built across multiple studies

Where imaging fits the broader question

MAIA's medical-image-analysis curriculum and mobility structure would let me bring a habit of subject-held-out evaluation to imaging problems, and learn directly what has to change when the signal becomes an image.
