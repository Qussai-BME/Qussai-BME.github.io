The reliability question

Can a biosignal model's reported accuracy be trusted once you check it under a protocol designed to expose failure, rather than flatter it? That question is the constant across all of my sEMG and EEG work.

What the evaluation has already shown

  • Strict leave-one-subject-out testing across NinaPro DB2, DB3, and DB7
  • A covariance and eigenvalue diagnosis of exactly why a tested domain-adaptation method failed
  • A protocol-locked next study (Lite-DAN), evaluated before results are read

Why Germany

A structured master's programme with advanced coursework, close supervision, and a research environment built around exactly this kind of methodological rigor — the missing piece between an independent evaluation and a properly supervised research programme.

What I'd study next

Making cross-subject evaluation genuinely useful for real biosignal systems, while keeping the protocol strict enough to actually expose where it breaks.
